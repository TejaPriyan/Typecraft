Privacy policy

How the website, local features and external services handle information.

Updated 27 October 2026

Text, fonts, audio and saved projects

The studio processes typed text, imported fonts, audio and rendered artwork in your browser. Saved projects use an IndexedDB database named typecraft on this browser profile. Clearing site data can remove those projects. The Project controls let you manage saved work; exported files are separate copies on your device.

An ordinary browser export creates a local download. In supported embedded environments, the studio can ask the host’s download integration to save a file; that host handles the requested save. Keep copies of important projects and exports.

Hosting and external services

When you load a page, the hosting service receives technical request information such as your IP address, browser details and requested URL. Operational logs and retention depend on the hosting provider and deployment. The studio loads Three.js from Cloudflare’s cdnjs service and fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers receive normal request data for those resources. Links to GitHub and other sites are governed by their operators’ policies.

Advertising and choices

This site identifies its publisher to Google AdSense for site review. Verification metadata and ads.txt do not themselves load advertisements. The pages in this release do not include an AdSense ad-serving script.

If Google advertising is enabled on a page, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers, IP addresses and device or browser information to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. This is separate from any local processing performed by the tool. Read how Google uses information from sites and apps and manage personalization through My Ad Center.

Where required, advertising must be accompanied by an appropriate consent mechanism before it is activated. Browser settings can remove stored site data and control cookies. Following an external link is governed by the destination service’s policy.

Questions and updates

Use the Contact page for questions about this site’s handling of information. Please describe the page and action involved. This notice should be updated whenever the site’s data processing or advertising configuration changes.
